What it is

An Internet-Draft is work in progress. Under RFC 2026, a draft that stays unchanged for more than six months is removed. Each draft also states the day it expires. An RFC is published once and keeps its number; a later RFC may update or obsolete it. RFCs are published in streams: IETF, IAB, IRTF, Independent Submission and Editorial.

Status

RFC 2026 is a Best Current Practice of October 1996, updated by later RFCs. RFC 9920, of February 2026, describes the RFC Editor model and obsoletes RFC 9280. Status checked on 18 November 2026.

Why a site for agents uses it

A card on this site about a draft carries the date on which the draft expires.
