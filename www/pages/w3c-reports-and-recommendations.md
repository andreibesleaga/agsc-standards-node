## What it is

On the Recommendation track a document moves from Working Draft to Candidate Recommendation, and then to Recommendation. A Community Group publishes reports, draft or final, and its reports are not W3C standards.

## Status

The W3C Process Document in force is dated 18 August 2025. Status checked on 18 November 2026.

## Why a site for agents uses it

Two documents on w3.org can have very different standing; each card on this site says which.
