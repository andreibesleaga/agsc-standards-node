## What it is

The C2PA technical specification binds a signed record of how a media file was made and edited, called a manifest, to the file.

## Status

Version 2.5 of the specification is dated October 2026. It is published by the Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity, a Joint Development Foundation project, not by the IETF or the W3C. Status checked on 18 November 2026.

## Why a site for agents uses it

A reader can check where an image or a recording came from.
