How a site says what automated readers may do, and how a reader says who it is.

Members

  • AI usage preferences (IETF drafts): Two IETF working-group drafts: a vocabulary for stating how content may be used by automated systems, and a way to attach it to content.
  • ODRL Information Model 2.2: A W3C policy language for stating how content and services may be used.
  • robots.txt (RFC 9309): The rules of the robots.txt file, with which a site tells crawlers what they may fetch.
  • TDM Reservation Protocol: A way for a rights holder to reserve text and data mining rights, in a file, a header or page metadata.
  • Web bot authentication (IETF working group): The IETF working group on how automated clients prove who they are to the sites they visit.
