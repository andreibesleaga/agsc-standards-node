Clusters

SummaryEvery published cluster of this node.

  • Act (1 item): How a page offers actions to an assistant.
  • Ask first (5 items): How a site says what automated readers may do, and how a reader says who it is.
  • Find (9 items): How a program finds what a site offers: fixed addresses, typed links and catalogues.
  • How it works (2 items): How to read the status of a document of the IETF or the W3C.
  • Trust the bytes (4 items): How a reader checks that a file is the one that was published, and who vouches for it.
  • Understand (6 items): How facts are written so that programs on different sites read them the same way.
  • Upkeep (9 items): The node's own work on one board: the decision, the card format, the gate and the open tasks.
  • Who made it (2 items): How a claim or a media file carries a record of its origin that can be checked.
